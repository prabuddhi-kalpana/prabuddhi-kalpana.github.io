Biography

With a Ph.D. in Chemical and Process Engineering, I specialize in process safety, environmental protection, and sustainable energy systems. My mission is to develop and implement technologies that support the net-zero carbon transition, fostering collaboration between academia and industry to create scalable, impactful solutions.
